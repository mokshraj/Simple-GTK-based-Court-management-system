Meerut murder case (March 2025)
Case details: Saurabh Rajput was allegedly drugged, stabbed, and dismembered by his wife, Muskan Rastogi, and her lover, Sahil Shukla. His body was concealed in a cement-filled drum. 
Key evidence:
Confession: Muskan and Sahil reportedly confessed to the crime, detailing how they sedated and killed Saurabh. They also admitted to dismembering his body and sealing it in a drum.
Autopsy report: A post-mortem report confirmed the brutality of the attack. It revealed multiple stab wounds to the heart, a severed neck and wrists, and a severely mutilated body.
Chemical analysis: The body was found encased in cement, indicating an attempt to destroy and conceal the evidence. The cement and other materials used to seal the drum were likely seized as physical evidence.
Statements from Saurabh's mother: The victim's mother told police that her six-year-old granddaughter reportedly told her, "Papa is in the drum".
Digital evidence:
WhatsApp messages and stories: After the murder, Muskan allegedly used Saurabh's phone to mislead his family by sending messages claiming he was busy. She also uploaded WhatsApp stories to create a false alibi.
Vacation photos and videos: Videos of Muskan and Sahil vacationing in Himachal Pradesh after the murder were leaked and reported, showing them partying and celebrating, further incriminating them.
Call and chat logs: Forensic analysis of Muskan's and Sahil's phones, as well as Saurabh's phone, provided a timeline of their communications before and after the murder.
Purchases: The police identified and questioned shopkeepers who sold items such as the drum, knives, and cement to the couple.
Forensic findings: Forensic teams reportedly found bloodstains on bedsheets, pillows, and in the bathroom, along with a suitcase the accused initially intended to use to dispose of the body. 
Alwar murder case (August 2025)
Case details: Hansraj was allegedly killed by his wife, Sunita, and her lover, Jitendra Sharma. His body was stuffed into a drum on the rooftop of their rented house. 
Key evidence:
Eyewitness testimony: The victim's eight-year-old son, Harshal, was the key eyewitness. He provided chilling details to the police, stating that his mother and "uncle" (Jitendra) had put his father's body in the drum. He also described the domestic violence his father inflicted on his mother.
Confession: After being apprehended, Sunita and Jitendra reportedly confessed to their roles in the murder.
Physical evidence: Police recovered the blue drum, a bedsheet in which the body was wrapped, and a sharp iron tool believed to have been used in the crime.
Confirmatory post-mortem: An autopsy confirmed that Hansraj's throat had been slit with a sharp weapon.
The drum and foul smell: The decomposed body was discovered in a sealed drum on the rooftop after the landlady complained of a foul smell.
Locating the suspects: The suspects were arrested at a brick kiln about 50 km away after an alert tip-off from locals who grew suspicious of their behavior.
