Meerut murder case (March 2025)
Victim
Sourav Rajput, a 29-year-old former merchant navy officer. 
Accused
Muskan Rastogi: Sourav's 27-year-old wife.
Sahil Shukla: Muskan's 25-year-old lover. 
Details of the crime
Motive: Muskan and Sahil, who were childhood friends, reconnected and began an affair in 2019. They conspired to kill Sourav so they could be together.
The plot: On March 4, Muskan allegedly laced Sourav's food with sedatives. Once he was unconscious, she called Sahil to their rented apartment.
The murder: Together, Muskan and Sahil stabbed Sourav in the heart with a knife. To conceal the body, they dismembered it, placed the parts in a blue plastic drum, and sealed it with cement. The autopsy later confirmed that Sourav's head was severed, and his wrists were cut off.
The cover-up: After the murder, the couple went on a vacation to Himachal Pradesh. They used Sourav's phone to post messages and photos on social media to make it appear as though he was alive and on a trip.
Investigation and arrest: Sourav's brother grew suspicious and filed a missing person report. After a foul smell was reported by neighbors, police raided the apartment and discovered the cement-sealed drum on March 18. Muskan and Sahil confessed after their arrest and were sent to judicial custody. 
Alwar murder case (August 2025)
Victim
Hansraj, a brick kiln worker from Uttar Pradesh. 
Accused
Sunita: Hansraj's wife.
Jitendra Sharma: Sunita's lover and the landlord's son. 
Details of the crime
Motive: Hansraj frequently drank with Jitendra. After discovering his wife's affair with Jitendra, Hansraj would regularly fight with Sunita, prompting the couple to conspire to kill him.
The murder: On the night of August 15, Hansraj, Sunita, and Jitendra were drinking together. A fight broke out, during which Hansraj was killed and his throat was slit.
The cover-up: The body was stuffed into a large blue drum on the rooftop, and salt was added to speed up decomposition.
Witness: The couple's 8-year-old son witnessed the murder and later told police how his father was killed and put in the drum.
Investigation and arrest: Neighbors complained of a foul smell, which led to the discovery of the body. Sunita and Jitendra initially fled with the children but were later arrested at a brick kiln about 50 km away.
